I already have a fully functional 3D timeline date-range selector in my web app.
Do NOT redesign the interaction or data model.
Your job is to make it look and feel excellent.

Core interaction concept

A horizontal timeline made of evenly spaced vertical 3D pillars / columns
Each pillar represents a fixed time slice (e.g. 1 day or 1 week)
Pillars extend upward from a base plane
Pillars visually respond when the user clicks and drags across them

Interaction behavior

Dragging the mouse horizontally selects a contiguous range of pillars
Selected pillars:
grow taller OR smoothly animate upward
shift color (e.g. neutral → accent color)
optionally emit subtle glow or light
Non-selected pillars remain muted
Hovering a pillar previews its date
On drag end, the component outputs:
start timestamp
end timestamp
